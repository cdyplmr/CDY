About

Im an electronic music maker. I make heavy, calming, peaceful, fast and ambient music.

I started making music in 2022, but i stopped since i wasnt really coming along well and most of my songs were abandond. Fast foward to 2026, ive come back to music with abit of practice. Im still learning and im too good.

I was inspired by Aphex Twin (Richard D. James), the weird, crazy, loud, and the peaceful, ambient, calming music inspired me to start trying to compose my own songs.

I also like listening to Old-Skool Happy Hardcore tracks by people like Luna-C (Christopher Howell), The Panacea, Seduction (John Kalkan), and more unknown composers. These people are usually in my other records that i just make for fun or end up in something for the future.

What music distributor do you use?

How long does it take for you to make a song?

It depends, some songs are easy, some songs are hard to make. It mostely depends on the genre.

What software do you use?

I use FL Studio (For most of my songs), Abelton (For the nice pianos), and i also use GarageBand for my passion projects.

What equiptment do you use?

I have a drumpad for drums and testing out kits, i have a akai keyboard for making melodies and chords, and i have a novation drumpad for my fast dnb songs.
